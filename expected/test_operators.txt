=== % (modulo) ===
17 % 5 = 2
10 % 3 = 1
9  % 3 = 0
=== prefix ++ / -- ===
++x = 6
x   = 6
--x = 5
x   = 5
=== postfix ++ / -- ===
y++ = 10
y   = 11
y-- = 11
y   = 10
=== += ==
100 += 50 → 150
=== -= ===
150 -= 30 → 120
=== *= ===
120 *= 2 → 240
=== /= ===
240 /= 4 → 60
=== %= ===
17 %= 5 → 2
=== float += / *= ===
1.5 += 0.5 → 2.000000
2.0 *= 3.0 → 6.000000
